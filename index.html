O.B. Montessori Center


Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center nurtures each child in the comprehensive method of Dr. Maria Montessori.

This is the O.B. Montessori Center of the Philippines!

We are a co-educational school, fostering students from pre-k to Grade 12. OBMC offers a viable alternative to international schools, blending both tradition and innovaton to prepare a child for life.

We aim to transform our students into independent individuals who can succeed, lead, and influence the future.

Explore our various programs and discover the remarkable community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to develop.
